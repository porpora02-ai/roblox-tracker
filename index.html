Questions

Before you sign up

Why can't I see any games?

Your tier decides which servers appear. Bronze shows servers under 10 players, Absolute shows everything. If you have no tier yet, open the Upgrade tab after signing in.

How does Roblox verification work?

Vantix gives you a code like VANTIX-4F2A9C. Paste it anywhere in your Roblox profile's About section, save on Roblox, then press Verify. We read your public profile to confirm the code is there, which proves the account is yours. You can delete the code afterwards.

Do I need to give you an email password?

No. We store an email address for your account record only — verification and password resets both run through your Roblox profile.

Are favorites synced across devices?

No. Favorites and appearance settings live in your browser's local storage, so each device keeps its own list. Your tier and account details are server-side.

How do I upgrade my tier?

DM #dr.muffinn on Discord or open a support ticket after purchasing. Tiers are applied manually from the owner panel.

Help

Common questions about tiers, tracking, and verification.

Why can't I see any games?

Your tier decides which servers appear. Bronze shows servers under 10 players, Absolute shows everything. If you have no tier yet, open the Upgrade tab.

How does the in-game panel work?

Bind your Roblox username under Tracking. When you join a whitelisted server, Vantix recognises you and your panel loads automatically inside the game.

How does Roblox verification work?

Vantix gives you a code like VANTIX-4F2A9C. Paste it anywhere in your Roblox profile's About section, save it on Roblox, then press Verify. We read your public profile to confirm the code is there, which proves the account is yours. You can delete the code afterwards.

Verify says the code isn't in my profile.

Make sure you saved the change on Roblox, and that you edited the About section on your profile rather than your status. Roblox can take a few seconds to update. If it still fails, generate a new code and try again.

I forgot my password.

Use "Forgot password?" on the login screen. You'll prove ownership with the same Roblox profile code, then set a new password. No email needed.

Are my favorites synced across devices?

No. Favorites and settings are stored in your browser's local storage, so each device keeps its own list.

What keyboard shortcuts are there?

Press Ctrl + K to open the command palette, / to focus search, ? for the full shortcut list, and Esc to close anything open.

How do I upgrade my tier?

DM #dr.muffinn on Discord or open a support ticket after purchasing. Tiers are applied manually from the owner panel.
